Why KLARDOSIS

1

Dosed at or below BfR maximum levels, not beyond them

Recent consumer tests found that half of 28 magnesium products exceeded the BfR recommendation of 250 mg per day. We take the opposite approach: every KLARDOSIS product is formulated at or below the BfR maximum levels from the start. Our magnesium, for example, stays at or below 250 mg per day. Look for our "Dosed within BfR guidance" mark on every pack. It marks our own dosing principle; it is not an approval or certification by the BfR.

2

Every batch, every result, public

Many people believe supplements are safety-tested before they go on sale. They are not. So we test them ourselves. Every pack carries a batch number and a QR code that links directly to the lab report for that batch, covering heavy metals and contaminants. Scan it, read it, decide for yourself.

3

Clean formulas with nothing to hide

No sweeteners. Minimal additives. Vegan wherever possible. A small, focused range: magnesium, vitamin D, a cultures-and-botanicals formula and a women's bundle. We don't try to sell you everything; we try to get the essentials right.

4

Honest words, including "you may not need this"

We only use health claims authorised by the EU, worded the way they were approved. No miracle promises, no disease claims, no influencer hype. And on every product page we tell you plainly who does not need the product. Your trust matters more to us than one extra sale.
