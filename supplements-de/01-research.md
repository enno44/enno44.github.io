# Market Research Report: Dietary Supplements (Nahrungsergänzungsmittel) in Germany

*Prepared by: research-agent | Date: 2026-11-20 | Status: desk research from public sources. Figures are labelled with their source. Where no public number was found, the report says so. "Estimate" marks the agent's own judgement.*

---

## 1. Market Trend Summary

**Market size: depends heavily on how the market is defined**
- **IQVIA (pharmacy incl. mail-order + mass market, Feb 2024–Feb 2025):** EUR 4.28 bn retail sales, **+9.9% YoY**. Pharmacies (store and mail-order) take about **80% by value (EUR 3.41 bn)**. Drugstores and supermarkets take about 20% (EUR 864 m). **IQVIA leaves out D2C shops, social commerce and marketplaces such as Amazon**, so the real total market is larger. [IQVIA infographic 05/2025](https://www.iqvia.com/-/media/iqvia/pdfs/germany/library/infographic/2025_iqvia_infografik_nahrungsergnzungsmittel_052025.pdf), cited via [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/drei-von-vier-personen-kaufen-regelmaessig-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-70191)
- **Commercial market reports give very different figures:** about USD 2.6 bn (Expert Market Research, 2024), USD 4.7 bn (Mordor, 2025, 7.25% CAGR), USD 9.3 bn (MarketsandMarkets, 2024, 5.8% CAGR to 2029) and USD 12.2 bn (MRFR, 2024). Each uses a different scope, so **do not compare them directly**. [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/dietary-supplements-market/GERMANY), [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/germany-nutritional-supplements-market), [MRFR](https://marketresearchfuture.com/reports/germany-dietary-supplements-market-47640)
- **Working assumption (estimate):** the retail and pharmacy core is about EUR 4.3 bn. Adding D2C, Amazon and sports nutrition plausibly brings it to **EUR 5–6 bn**, growing at **5–10% a year**. No single public source confirms this total.
- **Europe context:** IMARC puts Germany as the largest European country market, with about 25% of a USD 48.9 bn European market (2025). [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/europe-dietary-supplements-market)

**Growth drivers and category shifts**
- **Prevention, healthy ageing, longevity, self-care, sleep, energy and weight management** are the cited drivers. [Lebensmittel Praxis / NielsenIQ, 07/2025](https://lebensmittelpraxis.de/industrie-aktuell/45294-trends-im-sortiment-kategorie-otc-bietet-chancen.html)
- **Gut health is overtaking vitamins:** in German OTC mass-market data for 2024, stomach and digestion (EUR 419 m) passed vitamins and minerals (EUR 384 m). Magnesium is the largest single micronutrient at EUR 119 m (12 months to May 2025). (same source, NielsenIQ)
- **Protein is booming:** protein products in that panel grew from about EUR 79 m to EUR 136 m (2022–2024), with units up from 6.8 m to 11.1 m. (same source)
- **Sports and lifestyle nutrition is growing fastest:** The Quality Group (ESN, More Nutrition) reported **about EUR 850 m organic revenue in H1 2026, +40% YoY**, after passing EUR 1 bn annual revenue. [Lebensmittel Praxis 07/2026](https://lebensmittelpraxis.de/industrie-aktuell/49365-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-the-quality-group-steigert-umsatz-um-mehr-als-40-prozent.html)
- **Online share is rising:** online purchases of medicines, vitamins and supplements rose from 15% (2021) to 21% (2024) of people (IT.NRW / Destatis, via Verbraucherzentrale). In a 2025 BfR survey, 31% named the pharmacy and 22% named online as their main source. [BfR study](https://www.bfr.bund.de/assets/01_Ver%C3%B6ffentlichungen/Wissenschaftsberichte_deutsch/wissenschaftsbericht-sozialforschung-wahrnehmung-von-mikronaehrstoffen-in-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln.pdf)
- **Investor interest is high:** CVC bought about 55% of Sunday Natural in 2024 at a reported valuation of about EUR 800 m. Revenue was about EUR 100 m in 2022. [CVC](https://www.cvc.com/media/news/2024/2024-01-15-sunday-natural-secures-investment-from-cvc-fund-viii/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-01-15/cvc-is-said-to-agree-to-800-million-deal-for-sunday-natural)

**Regulatory and policy direction: more scrutiny, not less**
- **EU-wide binding maximum levels for vitamins and minerals are still missing.** They were foreseen in Directive 2002/46/EC. The Verbraucherzentrale expected a public Commission draft in **Q3 2026** and adoption in **Q1 2028**, followed by transition periods. We could not verify whether the draft has been published. [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/endlich-hoechstmengen-fuer-vitamine-und-mineralstoffe-46711)
- **Consumer groups (vzbv, Verbraucherzentralen, foodwatch) are active:** they bring lawsuits on health claims and influencer advertising (details in section 4).

**Takeaways on competition**
- **Low end:** dm and Rossmann private labels set a very low price floor for basic single nutrients such as vitamin D, magnesium and B12. **Competing on price there is hopeless.**
- **High end:** the pharmacy (Orthomol) and D2C (Sunday Natural) hold premium positions, built on pharmacist trust or clean labels.
- **Fastest growth:** influencer-driven sports and lifestyle brands (TQG), which carry regulatory and reputation risk.

## 3. Target Customer Profile

**Penetration:** 77% of respondents use supplements regularly, and more than half take them weekly. **Women use them more than men.** (Lebensmittelklarheit study, 05/2025, via [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/drei-von-vier-personen-kaufen-regelmaessig-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-70191))

**What people buy (Statista 2025):** vitamins 61%, minerals 36%, protein 28%, herbal 16%, plant-based such as algae oil 14%, animal-based such as fish oil or collagen 12%. The BfR found **magnesium and vitamin D** are used most, followed by vitamin C and calcium. (same source)

**Segments (our clustering, estimate)**
1. **Health-conscious mainstream (30–60, often women):** buys basics at dm or Rossmann. **Values price and convenience.** Wants immune support and "energy".
2. **Ageing and needs-specific buyers (50+):** shops at the pharmacy and asks for advice. Buys for joints, eyes, heart, bones. Pays a premium for trust.
3. **Fitness and lifestyle Gen Z and Millennials (18–34):** reached by influencers, TikTok and Instagram. Wants protein, creatine, flavours and community. YouGov finds 18–34-year-olds prefer protein-rich foods (17% vs 10% overall) and fortified foods (22% vs 17%) ([Lebensmittel Praxis](https://lebensmittelpraxis.de/industrie-aktuell/45294-trends-im-sortiment-kategorie-otc-bietet-chancen.html)). [Porsche Consulting](https://newsroom.porsche.com/de/2025/unternehmen/porsche-consulting-verbrauchergesundheit-generation-z-40815.html) also highlights Gen Z health spending.
4. **Clean-label and conscious buyers:** buys D2C. **Wants additive-free, vegan, sustainable and transparent products.**

**Pain points and decision factors**
- **Trust and information gaps:** 49% wrongly think products are safety-tested before sale, and 41% wrongly think maximum levels are legally binding (Verbraucherzentrale, see above). Consumers **lack the tools to judge quality**, which is an opportunity for a brand that offers real transparency.
- **Overdosing worries are growing:** Öko-Test 06/2026 found **half of 28 magnesium products** above the BfR recommendation of 250 mg a day. Stiftung Warentest recommended **only 2 of 25 vitamin D products** and judged all 18 kids' supplements tested in 2025 "superfluous" ([test.de](https://www.test.de/news/Nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-fuer-Kinder-im-Test-Stiftung-Warentest-deckt-fragwuerdige-Werbung-bei-Kinder-Vitaminen-auf-6332382-0/)). Media coverage is regular and critical.
- **Sceptical of hype:** influencer claims are widely debated, for example ZDF Magazin Royale on More Nutrition ([Händlerbund](https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/recht/rechtsfragen/139108-social-media-hype-more-nutrition-in-der-kritik-des-zdf-magazin-royale)).
- **Additives and sustainability:** Öko-Test criticises sweeteners (8 of 28 magnesium products) and unnecessary additives. Plastic packaging and vegan sourcing matter to the clean-label segment. No quantified sustainability survey for Germany was found.
- **Motives:** prevention and immune support are the main reasons. Users rate the benefit very high and largely ignore the risks (BfR survey, 04/2026).

## 4. Market Entry Barriers and Opportunities

### Regulatory framework (key points)
- **Legal status:** supplements are **foods, not medicines**. No pre-market approval is needed. The rules are EU Directive 2002/46/EC, implemented in Germany by the **Nahrungsergänzungsmittelverordnung (NemV)**, plus general food law (LFGB, EU 178/2002). [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/lebensmittel/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel/wie-sind-nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-geregelt-28253)
- **BVL notification (§5 NemV):** **each product** must be notified to the BVL, at the latest when first placed on the market. It is free and online via the federal portal. The acknowledgement is **not** a certificate that the product may legally be sold. [gesetze-im-internet.de](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/nemv/__5.html)
- **Labelling (§4 NemV + LMIV 1169/2011):**
  - Sales name "Nahrungsergänzungsmittel", nutrient categories, daily dose and % NRV.
  - Mandatory warnings: do not exceed the recommended dose; not a substitute for a varied diet; keep out of reach of children.
  - Labels in **German**.
  - Online shops must show the mandatory information before purchase (Art. 14 LMIV). [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/nahrungsergaenzungsmittel-werbung-verkauf.html)
- **Health claims (EC 1924/2006, Union list in Reg. 432/2012):** only **authorised claims, worded close to the list**, are allowed. No disease claims. "Botanical" claims remain on hold. **Influencer content counts as advertising** and is a frequent source of legal action. In single studies, about 90% of influencer statements on supplements were not permissible. [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/node/28499), [foodwatch report 06/2025](https://www.foodwatch.org/fileadmin/-DE/Themen/Gesundheitsschwindel/2025-06_Health-Claims-Report.pdf)
- **Maximum amounts:** Germany has **no binding national limits**. Authorities and courts use the **BfR maximum-level proposals (updated 02/2024)** as a benchmark, for example magnesium 250 mg a day from supplements. **Mutual recognition** allows products that are legal elsewhere in the EU, but in practice German retailers and testers enforce the BfR values. [BfR 2024](https://mobil.bfr.bund.de/cm/343/aktualisierte-hoechstmengenvorschlaege-fuer-vitamine-und-mineralstoffe-in-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln-und-angereicherten-lebensmitteln-2024.pdf)
- **Novel Foods (EU 2015/2283):** many trendy ingredients need approval, such as some mushroom extracts, CBD and new botanicals. EU controls found unauthorised novel foods in **55% of online product offers checked**. [Verbraucherzentrale](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/node/28499)
- **Other rules:**
  - Packaging law (VerpackG/LUCID registration).
  - Organic certification if claiming "bio".
  - Sports products may need doping-free testing (e.g. **Kölner Liste**). This is our recommendation, not a legal requirement.

### Distribution
- **Drugstores (dm, Rossmann, Müller):** the biggest volume channel, but led by private labels. Listing is hard, and branded products need proof of demand. (Mintel via [Lebensmittel Praxis](https://lebensmittelpraxis.de/drogerieartikel/41059-gesundheitsprodukte-warum-drogerien-im-verkauf-von-nahrungsergaenzungsmitteln-immer-noch-dominieren.html))
- **Pharmacies, including mail-order (Shop Apotheke, DocMorris):** about 80% of IQVIA value. Entry needs a pharmacy wholesaler (Großhandel), field force or pharmacist detailing, and a PZN. **High cost, high trust.**
- **Amazon:** a major channel for D2C brands such as Natural Elements. **No public Amazon share for German supplements was found.** Reviews and rankings decide, and price pressure is intense.
- **D2C and influencers:** the fastest route to scale (TQG, Sunday Natural), but with high customer-acquisition costs and legal risk on claims. The vzbv found manipulative design in all 18 apps it checked, including Amazon, TikTok and Temu (2025).

### Barriers
1. **Price floor set by drugstore private labels** for basic single nutrients.
2. **Trust deficit and testing culture:** a poor Öko-Test or Stiftung Warentest rating can damage a brand quickly.
3. **Claims law limits marketing language.** Consumer groups and competitors use cease-and-desist letters (Abmahnungen) actively.
4. **Costly access to the pharmacy channel**, and gatekeeping in drugstore listings.
5. **Crowded D2C space and high paid-social costs** (estimate: high CAC). foodspring's exit shows the risk.
6. **Regulatory uncertainty:** EU maximum levels from about 2028 may force reformulation.

### Opportunities
1. **"Science-dosed and transparent" positioning.** Formulate below BfR maximums by design and publish batch lab certificates (heavy metals, contaminants). Use only authorised claims and communicate "no overdosing" clearly. This answers the main public criticism directly, and **no major player owns this position clearly** (estimate).
2. **Gut health and the microbiome:** the largest and fastest-shifting OTC segment in 2024 data, larger than vitamins and minerals.
3. **Protein and creatine for 18–34s,** using compliant influencer marketing. Being the "clean, legally solid" alternative to TQG could stand out after the More Nutrition controversies.
4. **Needs-based bundles** for women's health (cycle, menopause), 50+ and longevity, sold as D2C subscriptions to give recurring revenue.
5. **Sustainability:** refill or glass packaging, vegan and minimal additives (no sweeteners), aimed at the clean-label segment.
6. **Channel sequence (estimate):** D2C plus Amazon to prove demand and collect reviews → drugstore listing (dm and Rossmann list strong D2C brands) → pharmacy only for clinically supported products.
