### Core selling points

**1. Dosed at or below BfR maximum levels, not beyond them**
Recent consumer tests found that half of 28 magnesium products exceeded the BfR recommendation of 250 mg per day. We take the opposite approach: every KLARDOSIS product is formulated at or below the BfR maximum levels from the start. Our magnesium, for example, stays at or below 250 mg per day. Look for our "Dosed within BfR guidance" mark on every pack. It marks our own dosing principle; it is not an approval or certification by the BfR.

**2. Every batch, every result, public**
Many people believe supplements are safety-tested before they go on sale. They are not. So we test them ourselves. Every pack carries a batch number and a QR code that links directly to the lab report for that batch, covering heavy metals and contaminants. Scan it, read it, decide for yourself.

**3. Clean formulas with nothing to hide**
No sweeteners. Minimal additives. Vegan wherever possible. A small, focused range: magnesium, vitamin D, a cultures-and-botanicals formula and a women's bundle. We don't try to sell you everything; we try to get the essentials right.

**4. Honest words, including "you may not need this"**
We only use health claims authorised by the EU, worded the way they were approved. No miracle promises, no disease claims, no influencer hype. And on every product page we tell you plainly who does *not* need the product. Your trust matters more to us than one extra sale.

## Presentation Key Points

*Order: Problem → Solution → Differentiation → Benefit*

1. **Problem: the market sells "more", not "right".** Öko-Test found half of 28 magnesium products above the BfR recommendation of 250 mg/day. Stiftung Warentest recommended only 2 of 25 vitamin D products.
2. **Problem: consumers can't judge quality.** 49% wrongly believe supplements are safety-tested before sale. 41% wrongly believe maximum levels are legally binding.
3. **Solution: KLARDOSIS, "the right amount, proven."** A German supplement brand dosed at or below BfR maximums by design, with each batch's lab certificate published online via a QR code.
4. **Differentiation: correct dose and per-batch proof.** Pure claims are already owned in the market; "correct dose" is not. No major player clearly owns "science-dosed and transparent" (strategy estimate).
5. **Differentiation: legally solid by design.** Only EU Union-list claims, clean formulas (no sweeteners, minimal additives) and honest guidance on who doesn't need a product. We are the opposite of high-dose and hype brands.
6. **Benefit for customers: trust they can verify.** No more guessing which product is safe to take daily; the proof is one scan away.
7. **Benefit for the business: built for what comes next.** Formulating below BfR values today should reduce the risk of forced reformulation when EU binding maximum levels arrive (expected around 2028).
8. **Path to market: D2C first, then scale.** Own shop with subscriptions and content-led growth, then Amazon, then dm/Rossmann listing once demand is proven.

## Compliance Notes for CEO / Developer

- **No product benefit claims are written into this copy on purpose.** Any statement about what magnesium, vitamin D, the cultures-and-botanicals formula or the women's bundle ingredients *do* must be copied from the EU Union list (Reg. 432/2012) and reviewed by a food-law firm before going live (strategy Phase 0).
- **Product names (QA fix):** in customer-facing copy, name products by what they contain, never by a health outcome. Do not use "gut health" or "women's health" as product names: without an authorised specific claim next to them they are non-specific health claims (Art. 10(3) Reg. 1924/2006). Final product names are set after the Phase 0 food-law review.
- **Cultures-and-botanicals formula (internally: gut product):** probiotic and botanical ingredients have limited authorised claims and possible Novel Food issues. Keep its copy descriptive only until regulatory review is done.
- **Do not state or imply** that KLARDOSIS has passed or will pass Öko-Test or Stiftung Warentest. Test results cited above refer to the market, not to our products.
- **"At or below BfR maximum levels"** is the standard dosing wording; use it everywhere. It describes a recommendation, not a legal limit; do not call it "legally approved dosing" or "BfR-compliant". The pack mark "Dosed within BfR guidance" is our own self-declared principle, not a BfR approval; have the label wording checked in the Phase 0 claims review.
- **Unverified:** whether competitors (especially Sunday Natural) already publish batch certificates. Avoid "the only brand that..." wording until this is checked.
- **Trademark** for "KLARDOSIS" is not yet checked.
