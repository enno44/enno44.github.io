Coral boxes are Team A, blue boxes Team B; the swatches show the kit colour each team was learned from. Grey means the colour matched neither team (often referees or spectators). The yellow ring is the ball. Possession goes to the team of the player closest to the ball.

Light version vs. full pipeline

The browser demo uses COCO-SSD, the small TensorFlow.js model from the course. The full pipeline runs locally on a laptop and is much stronger at finding the small ball.
